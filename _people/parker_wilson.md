Dr. Wilson is an Assistant Professor and principal investigator in the Division of Diagnostic Innovation within the Department of Pathology and Laboratory Medicine at the Perelman School of Medicine. He is a renal and molecular pathologist whose lab uses single-cell, spatial, and computational approaches to study chronic kidney disease.
