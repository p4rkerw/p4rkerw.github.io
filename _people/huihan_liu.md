Huihan is a master's student in Bioengineering at Penn and joined the Wilson Lab in October 2025. She analyzes bulk and single-cell RNA-seq data from rat models of kidney injury and aging, helped establish the lab's renal irradiation workflows, and develops cell-penetrating peptide and CRISPR ribonucleoprotein approaches for genome editing. Huihan received her B.S. in Biochemistry from the University of California, San Diego.
