Amit is a postdoctoral fellow who leads translational studies of epithelial injury, somatic mosaicism, and DCLK1 biology in kidney disease. He builds CRISPR-Cas9 kidney injury models, digital PCR assays for somatic copy number alterations, and rodent models of kidney injury. Amit earned his PhD in Medical Biotechnology from Gurukula Kangri University in India, studying the genetics of type 2 diabetes, before joining Penn in 2023.
