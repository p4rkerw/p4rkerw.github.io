Roy is a PhD student in Bioengineering at Penn and joined the Wilson Lab in August 2025. His research integrates single-cell and spatial transcriptomics to uncover molecular drivers of repair after acute kidney injury. Roy graduated from Duke University with degrees in Biomedical Engineering and Computer Science (AI/ML concentration), where he worked on spatial omics integration, tissue-engineered vascular grafts, and acoustofluidic droplet sorting.
