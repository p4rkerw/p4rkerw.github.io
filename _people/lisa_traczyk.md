Lisa is a research specialist in the Wilson Lab. She first volunteered in the lab in October 2023 and joined full-time in January 2025. Lisa runs digital PCR assays for somatic mosaicism, cell culture, immunofluorescence, and single-cell sequencing experiments, and she mentors the lab's student interns. She received her B.S. in Biology from Rutgers University–Camden, where she did honors research on drought tolerance in *Arabidopsis* and on memory in Alzheimer's disease model flies.
