Yongqi is a master's student in Bioengineering at Penn who develops machine learning methods for protein interactions and cellular perturbation modeling. He built [P3Bind](https://github.com/yongqih/P3Bind), an interaction-aware framework for PDZ–PBM binding affinity prediction and peptide design. He is also first author of a 2026 [preprint](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.08.21.746243v1) showing why virtual-cell models struggle to generalize to unseen interventions.
