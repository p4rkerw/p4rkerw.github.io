Dr. Wilson was an invited speaker at the American Diabetes Association 84th Scientific Sessions. He reviewed sequencing-based and imaging-based spatial transcriptomics, their trade-offs in resolution, sparsity and cell segmentation, and how spatial profiling identifies pro-fibrotic and pro-inflammatory microenvironments in diabetic kidney disease.
