Our preprint on multimodal single-cell sequencing of human diabetic kidney disease is on bioRxiv. We paired snRNA-seq and snATAC-seq of early diabetic kidneys to show how changes in chromatin accessibility and genetic background shape the response of the proximal tubule to diabetes.

The work was later published in *Nature Communications* (2022). Read the [published paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36068241/), browse the [data on GEO (GSE195460)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE195460), or reproduce the analysis with our [containerized workflow](https://github.com/p4rkerw/Wilson_Muto_NComm_2022).
