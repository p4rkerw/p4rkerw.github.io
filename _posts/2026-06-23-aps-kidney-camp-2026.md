Dr. Wilson taught at the American Physiological Society Kidney Camp. He introduced [CHASM](/software/), the lab's new tool for detecting somatic copy number alterations from single-cell ATAC data, and showed that injured proximal tubule accumulates alterations on chromosomes 3, 7, 10 and X with age.
