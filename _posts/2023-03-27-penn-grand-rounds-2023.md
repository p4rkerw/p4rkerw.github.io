Dr. Wilson presented Grand Rounds at the University of Pennsylvania. Along with the lab's work on diabetic kidney disease, he presented new findings on mosaic loss of the Y chromosome (LOY) in the kidney: LOY is enriched in the kidney cortex, increases with age, and is associated with DNA damage and cell cycle pathways.
