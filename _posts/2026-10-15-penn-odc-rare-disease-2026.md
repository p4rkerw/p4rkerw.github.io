Dr. Wilson presented at the Penn Orphan Disease Center on targeting DCLK1 in polycystic kidney disease. Single-cell sequencing found increased DCLK1 in injured proximal tubule and fibroblasts in human ADPKD, and the lab is developing PKD mouse models in collaboration with the KUMC rodent core.
