Dr. Wilson spoke at ASN Kidney Week 2026 about somatic chromosomal mosaicism in chronic kidney disease. He described how CHASM detects copy number alterations in single cells, how injured proximal tubule accumulates alterations with age in both human and rat kidney, and the lab's work on DCLK1 as a therapeutic target.
