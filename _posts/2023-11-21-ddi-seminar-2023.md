Dr. Wilson presented to the Division of Diagnostic Innovation in Penn Pathology. The talk introduced new digital PCR assays for somatic copy number variation, which detect LOY and correlate with single-cell estimates, and scalable co-sequencing of RNA and DNA in single cells (DEFND-seq).
