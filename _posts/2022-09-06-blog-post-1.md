Our manuscript on multimodal single-cell sequencing in human diabetic kidney disease is now published in *Nature Communications*. Single-nucleus RNA and ATAC sequencing found that diabetic kidney disease is associated with an increase in injured VCAM1+ proximal tubule cells and leukocytes. Changes in chromatin accessibility implicate glucocorticoid and insulin receptor signaling. Allele-specific analysis points to genetic background as a modifier of disease.
