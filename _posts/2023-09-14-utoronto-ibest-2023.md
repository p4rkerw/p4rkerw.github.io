Dr. Wilson gave an invited lecture at the Institute for Biomedical Engineering, Science and Technology (iBEST) at the University of Toronto. He discussed single-cell multiomics of diabetic kidney disease, germline variation as a disease modifier, and spatial profiling with 10x Visium to find pro-fibrotic microenvironments in CKD.
