Dr. Wilson presented the lab's growing program on somatic mosaicism at the Penn/CHOP Kidney Innovation Center. Topics included somatic SNV burden and mutational signatures from snATAC-seq, detection of copy number alterations in single cells, and the relationship between loss of the Y chromosome, age, and proximal tubule injury.
