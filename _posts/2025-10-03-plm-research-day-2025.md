Dr. Wilson presented at the Penn Pathology and Laboratory Medicine Research Day. Injured proximal tubule cells are found in healthy kidney and increase with aging and CKD. They carry more somatic copy number alterations, are the most likely to have lost the Y chromosome, and express the oncogene DCLK1, a candidate therapeutic target.
