Congratulations to first author Y. Lin and Wilson lab PhD candidate Gary Wang on their paper in *Nature Communications*, a collaboration with the Zhang lab. TraceBind corrects sample-specific Tn5 cleavage bias by fine-tuning a pretrained cleavage model on mitochondrial reads. This enables more accurate transcription factor footprinting in both bulk and single-cell ATAC-seq.

[manuscript link](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42265104/)
