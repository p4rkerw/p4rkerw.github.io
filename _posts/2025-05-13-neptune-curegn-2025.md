Dr. Wilson presented a tutorial to the NEPTUNE and CureGN pathology working groups on sequencing and spatial methods for kidney tissue. The session covered single-cell workflows, KPMP data integration, long-read isoform analysis, and sequencing- and imaging-based spatial transcriptomics.
