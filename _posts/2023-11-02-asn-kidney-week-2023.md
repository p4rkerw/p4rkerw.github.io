Kidney Week came to Philadelphia in 2023, and Dr. Wilson gave an educational talk on single-cell modalities. He compared scRNA-seq and snRNA-seq platforms, explained chromatin accessibility profiling, and showed how large-scale integration of single-cell and spatial datasets by the Kidney Precision Medicine Project identifies fibrotic microenvironments in CKD.
