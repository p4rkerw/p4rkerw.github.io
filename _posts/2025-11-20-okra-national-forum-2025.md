Dr. Wilson spoke at the George M. O'Brien Kidney Resource Alliance National Forum. He showed that open chromatin in injured proximal tubule is enriched for heritability of CKD, that injured proximal tubule is more likely to carry gains on chromosomes 3, 7, 10, 12, 19 and X, and described rat models of aging and adenine-induced CKD used to study DCLK1.
