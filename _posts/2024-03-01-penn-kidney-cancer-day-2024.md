Dr. Wilson spoke at Penn Kidney Cancer Day about somatic mosaicism in the non-neoplastic kidney, including large gains on chromosomes 7, 10 and X, and digital PCR assays showing that sex chromosome aneuploidy increases with age and is tissue-specific.
