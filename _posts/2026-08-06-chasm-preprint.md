Our collaborative preprint with the [Zhang lab](https://nzhanglab.github.io/) is now on bioRxiv! Led by Xinyi (Emilia) Chen with Wilson lab PhD candidate Gary Wang, *High-Specificity Detection of Chromosomal Mosaicism Reveals Cell-Type-Specific Genomic Alteration Patterns in Aging Tissues* introduces **CHASM**, a method that detects chromosomal copy number alterations from single-cell ATAC-seq data.

In 99 human kidney samples, CHASM finds mosaic chromosomal alterations enriched in injury-associated VCAM1-high proximal tubule cells. It also detects the age-associated emergence of alterations in cancer-relevant regions, including chromosome 3 and chromosome 7, in ostensibly normal cells.
