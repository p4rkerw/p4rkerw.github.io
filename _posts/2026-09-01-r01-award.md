The Wilson lab has been awarded an R01 from the NIDDK, *DCLK1 isoform switching and proximal tubule injury in kidney disease* (2026–2031). Aging and CKD shift which DCLK1 isoforms injured proximal tubule cells express, and this may change how DCLK1 interacts with PDZ scaffold proteins to promote disease. The project will:

- Quantify DCLK1 isoforms in animal models with isoform-specific qPCR and long-read sequencing.
- Test DCLK1 kinase inhibitors.
- Map inflammatory signaling from DCLK1+ injured proximal tubule cells in human kidney with single-cell-resolution spatial transcriptomics.
